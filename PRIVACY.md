# Privacy Policy for Acta Diurna

**Acta Diurna** is an open-source, local-first task manager. Your privacy is a fundamental part of the application's design.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
*   **Local Storage Only**: All your tasks, projects, and contexts are stored exclusively on your device in the `todo.txt` and `done.txt` files.
*   **No Personal Data Collection**: The application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage statistics, or crash reports to any external servers.
*   **No Accounts**: You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use this application.

## 2. Permissions
*   **Storage Access**: The app requires permission to access your device's storage only to read and write your task files. 
*   **Internet Access**: The app does not request or use internet permissions. Your data never leaves your device through this application.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Acta Diurna does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 4. Open Source
The source code of this application is publicly available for auditing at: [https://github.com/dtoffe/ActaDiurna](https://github.com/dtoffe/ActaDiurna)
