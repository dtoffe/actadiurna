# Acta Diurna

**Plan your day like a Roman Emperor.**

Acta Diurna (**akta diˈurna**) is a minimalist offline plain `todo.txt` editor for Android. It focuses on quick task management, efficient filtering, and structured sorting, all while respecting your privacy.

## Features

- **Standard todo.txt Support**: Full compatibility with the [todo.txt format](http://todotxt.org/).
- **Minimalist & Offline**: No internet permission required. Your data never leaves your device.
- **Efficient UI**:
    - **Compact Layout**: See more tasks at once with a streamlined text-focused flow.
    - **Smart Search**: Quick filtering by text, @contexts, or +projects.
    - **One-Tap Sorting**: Quickly reorder by Priority, Project, Context, or Alphabetically.
- **Intelligent Task Management**:
    - **Visual Highlighting**: Overdue tasks are clearly marked in red.
    - **Group Colorization**: Tasks are subtly color-grouped when sorting by Project or Context.
    - **Selection Flow**: Select a task to perform quick actions like changing priority or toggling tags.
- **Modern Android Support**:
    - **Adaptive Icon**: Beautiful Roman-themed icon that fits your system style.
    - **Backwards Compatibility**: Supports Android 5.0 (API 21) and up.
- **Free & Open Source**: Licensed under the MIT License. No ads, no tracking, no "Pro" features.
