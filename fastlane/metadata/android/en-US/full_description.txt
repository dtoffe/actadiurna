Acta Diurna is a minimalist, open-source task manager that uses the classic todo.txt format.

Designed for speed and simplicity, it allows you to manage your tasks without the clutter of traditional apps.

Key Features:
• Full todo.txt format support: priorities, contexts, projects, and due dates.
• Powerful search and filtering.
• Advanced sorting: by priority, alphabet, project, or context.
• Archive completed tasks to a separate done.txt file.
• Import and export your data easily.
• Open-source and privacy-focused.

Stay productive with a tool that respects your workflow.
